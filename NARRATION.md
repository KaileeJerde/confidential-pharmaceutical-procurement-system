## Opening & Problem Statement

Welcome to **PrivacyPharma**, a confidential pharmaceutical procurement system built with **FHEVM**.

Traditional blockchain auctions expose all bids publicly, revealing **sensitive pricing strategies** and buyer needs.

This creates unfair advantages and privacy concerns.

---

## FHEVM Solution

PrivacyPharma uses **Fully Homomorphic Encryption** to solve this.

The smart contract compares bids and finds the **best supplier** while all prices remain **encrypted**.

No one sees the actual values, not even the contract itself.

---

## Live Demonstration - Order Creation

Let me show you how it works.

First, a buyer creates an order with encrypted details: **drug ID, quantity, and maximum budget**.

All values are encrypted before being stored on-chain.

---

## Live Demonstration - Bidding

Three verified suppliers submit their **encrypted bids**.

Supplier A bids forty-five hundred, Supplier B bids forty-two hundred, and Supplier C bids forty-eight hundred.

But remember, these prices are **completely encrypted** during the auction.

---

## Live Demonstration - Matching

Now the buyer matches the order.

The smart contract uses **FHE operations** to compare encrypted quantities, validate budgets, and find the **lowest price**.

All computations happen on encrypted data.

Supplier B wins with the best bid.

---

## Fulfillment & FHEVM Concepts

The winning supplier fulfills the order, triggering **asynchronous decryption**.

Only now are the order details revealed to the winner.

This demonstrates **four key FHEVM concepts**: **encryption** of sensitive data, **access control** with granular permissions, **private computation** using FHE operations, and **public decryption** with callback patterns.
